# Safe Method Quality Governance Note
**Draft, Benchmark, and Issue-Ready Decision Rules**
Version: 2026-03-28

---

## Purpose

This note explains who decides when a Safe Method output is:
- draft-quality
- benchmark-quality
- issue-ready

It exists to keep quality decisions clear, repeatable, and commercially safe.

The main principle is simple:

**Safe Method can generate and improve drafts, but people remain responsible for benchmark acceptance and issue-ready sign-off.**

---

## 1. Quality States

### Draft-Quality

The output is useful as a working draft, but still requires review, correction, or confirmation before reliance.

Typical characteristics:
- structure is usable
- major logic is present
- uncertainty is visible
- open items still exist
- project facts may still need confirmation

### Benchmark-Quality

The output is strong enough to act as an internal benchmark draft for review and comparison.

Typical characteristics:
- benchmark case is materially satisfied
- section logic is coherent
- the main product mode is working
- remaining gaps are known and visible
- useful for consultant review and refinement

### Issue-Ready

The output is suitable to be issued under professional responsibility for a real project.

Typical characteristics:
- critical project facts are confirmed
- issue-gating defects are closed
- required evidence and approvals exist
- open items are resolved or explicitly managed
- a responsible human is prepared to stand behind the document

---

## 2. Decision Ownership

### Safe Method

Safe Method is responsible for:
- generating the output
- applying internal issue gates
- applying benchmark scoring
- exposing uncertainty and open items
- improving reusable logic through LBV and benchmark refinement

Safe Method is **not** the final authority on issue-ready status.

### Internal Product / Benchmark Owner

The internal owner is responsible for deciding:
- whether the output is good enough to count as benchmark-quality
- whether a refinement loop should continue
- whether a finding is:
  - a reusable rule
  - a case-specific fix
  - a product decision
  - a defer item

This is usually the person running the LBV flywheel or directing Claude Code refinement.

### Expert Reviewer

An expert reviewer, such as an experienced WHS consultant or a specialist review agent, is responsible for:
- testing whether the output is trustworthy in real review conditions
- identifying where trust drops
- identifying where product claims exceed actual output quality
- confirming whether the benchmark draft is commercially useful

Expert review helps determine benchmark strength, but it does not replace issue sign-off.

### Responsible Issuing Person

The issuing consultant, manager, or authorised human reviewer is responsible for:
- confirming project facts
- confirming evidence and approvals
- deciding whether the document is safe and defensible to issue
- accepting accountability for the issued output

Only this role can move a document from benchmark-quality to issue-ready.

---

## 3. Decision Rules By Stage

### Moving to Draft-Quality

A document may be treated as draft-quality when:
- the product path runs successfully
- the output is structurally usable
- major logic is present
- uncertainty is visible rather than hidden

Decision owner:
- Safe Method plus internal operator

### Moving to Benchmark-Quality

A document may be treated as benchmark-quality when:
- benchmark/reference comparison is materially satisfactory
- expert review indicates the output is useful for real review work
- the major product claim is supported by actual output
- remaining weaknesses are visible and bounded

Decision owner:
- internal product / benchmark owner
- informed by expert review

### Moving to Issue-Ready

A document may be treated as issue-ready only when:
- issue-gate failures are closed
- critical placeholders are resolved
- required approvals, surveys, sign-offs, and validations exist
- project facts are confirmed
- a responsible human reviewer is willing to issue it

Decision owner:
- responsible issuing person

Safe Method must not imply that benchmark-quality automatically means issue-ready.

---

## 4. Minimum Governance Checks

### For Draft-Quality
- usable structure
- no fatal generation failure
- visible uncertainty/open items

### For Benchmark-Quality
- benchmark comparison completed
- expert or consultant-style review completed where warranted
- main weaknesses identified
- refinement decision recorded:
  - continue and refine
  - narrow scope
  - pause/defer
  - close benchmark stream

### For Issue-Ready
- issue gate passed
- project-critical fields completed
- method-validity checks completed where relevant
- emergency/rescue requirements completed where relevant
- approvals/evidence/sign-offs confirmed where relevant
- accountable issuer identified

---

## 5. Product-Mode Application

### SWMS

Draft-quality:
- useful consultant draft

Benchmark-quality:
- suitable for benchmark comparison and consultant review

Issue-ready:
- task, control, emergency, evidence, and responsibility details are confirmed

### RA

Draft-quality:
- usable project-specific risk draft

Benchmark-quality:
- classification, HRCW logic, and grouped risk structure are materially strong

Issue-ready:
- project facts and review-critical assumptions are confirmed by a responsible reviewer

### Project WHS Benchmark / Control Pack

Draft-quality:
- usable scaffold for project review

Benchmark-quality:
- package extraction, HRCW mapping, hold points, and risk crosswalk are strong enough for consultant benchmark use

Issue-ready:
- package facts, evidence, approvals, and project-specific confirmations are closed by the responsible issuing party

---

## 6. Governance Principle

The quality ladder is:

**generated output -> draft-quality -> benchmark-quality -> issue-ready**

These are not the same state.

The governance rule is:

**Safe Method may automate generation and improvement, but people remain responsible for benchmark acceptance and issue-ready sign-off.**
