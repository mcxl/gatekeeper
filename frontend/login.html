WHY SIMPLE SWMS IS DIFFERENT

Always Referenced. Never Guessed.

AI can hallucinate. It can generate plausible-looking standard numbers that don’t exist, or reference legislation that has been superseded. On a safety document reviewed by a regulator or handed to a principal contractor, that is a serious problem.

Safe Method solves this with a Verified Reference Layer — a curated registry of current standards and codes of practice across all five jurisdictions we support. Every document is checked against this registry before you see it. Unverified citations are stripped. Only real, current references make it through.
