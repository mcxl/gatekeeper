**Crack Stitching with Thor Helical Bars**
*Scope: Cut slots into masonry, install Thor helical bars, and grout to stitch cracks in brick or block walls.*

**HOLD POINT — do not start until:**
- Confirm crack width, depth, and cause before cutting slots — get approval to proceed.
- Inspect grouted slots before pointing — ensure full bar embedment and no voids.
- Final check: confirm all bars are set and pointing is flush before sign-off.

**Controls:**
- Use a wet-cut grinder or SDS drill to cut slots at correct depth and spacing.
- Check for hidden services before cutting — use a cable and pipe detector.
- Set up full dust controls: water suppression, vacuum shroud, or both.
- Wear a P2 respirator when cutting or mixing grout in enclosed or low-air spaces.
- Barricade the work zone to keep others clear of flying debris and dust.
- Mix grout to maker's ratio — do not add extra water.
- Inject grout from the back of the slot forward to avoid air pockets.
- Insert helical bars while grout is wet — push to full depth.
- Check bar spacing matches the engineer's or maker's layout drawing.
- Allow grout to cure to maker's time before pointing or loading the wall.
- Store helical bars flat and dry to prevent kinks or rust before use.
- Dispose of grout waste and slurry as per site and local rules.
- Inspect tools before use — check discs, chucks, and leads for damage.
- Keep bystanders at least 3 m back from any cutting work.
- Report any new or widening cracks found during work to the supervisor at once.

**STOP WORK if:**
- Crack is active or still moving — stop and get structural advice.
- Hidden services are struck or suspected — stop all cutting at once.
- Grout will not bond or slots are wet from rising damp — stop work.
- Wall shows signs of instability or bulging — evacuate and report.
- Dust controls fail and no respiratory protection is available — stop cutting.

**Admin:**
- Confirm engineer or maker's specification is on site before starting.
- Complete a pre-start check of all tools and PPE each day.
- Record bar locations, grout batch numbers, and cure times in the site log.
- Brief all workers on the task hazards and controls before starting.
- Review MSDS for grout product — keep a copy on site.

**PPE:**
- P2 respirator
- Safety glasses or goggles
- Face shield when cutting
- Hearing protection
- Cut-resistant gloves
- Steel-capped boots
- Hard hat
- Hi-vis vest

**Who checks:**
- SUP — Confirm spec is on site, approve hold points, check grout and bar records daily.
- WKR — Follow cutting, grouting, and bar-install steps, wear PPE, and report issues at once.
